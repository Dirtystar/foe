Questions

Do I need a licence to try it?

No. The free tier farms 1 world with the full engine. Buy a plan only when you want more worlds at once.

How does the licence work?

After buying, you get a key by email. Paste it into the app and press Save. It's checked offline — no login, no phone-home. Your plan sets how many worlds you can run at once.

Which platforms?

Windows, macOS and Linux. The app manages its own browser, so there's nothing else to set up.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime, from the receipt/checkout portal. You keep access until the period ends.
