# Forge GBG Farmer — User Guide

Farm Guild Battlegrounds (GBG) in Forge of Empires automatically, across up to 8 worlds at
once. You open the app, pick your worlds, set how far to fight, press **Start**, and walk away.

---

## What it does

- Opens its own browser window and drives Guild Battlegrounds for you.
- On each world it enters GBG, picks provinces, and fights them with **Auto-battle**.
- Stops fighting a world when it reaches the **attrition limit** you set.
- Runs several worlds **at the same time** (how many depends on your plan).
- Heals itself: if a world gets stuck, it reloads and re-enters GBG on its own.

It does **not** touch anything else in your city and never spends diamonds.

---

## Install & first run

1. Install the app (see the download page for your platform — Windows, macOS, Linux).
2. Start it. You'll see the **Forge GBG Farmer** window.
3. Click **Open browser**. A dedicated browser window opens.
4. In that window, **log in to each Forge world** you want to farm (cz1…cz8) and open
   Guild Battlegrounds once so the game knows you're in the season.
5. Back in the app, tick the worlds to farm, then press **Start farming**.

> Keep that browser window as the app opened it. It's sized so the app can find the GBG
> entrance reliably — don't maximize or resize it.

---

## The main window

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Licence key** | Paste your key here and press **Save**. Empty = free tier (1 world). |
| **Farm** (tick) | Turn a world on/off. You can enable up to your plan's number of worlds. |
| **Browser tab** | Which browser tab is that world (defaults to the world name, e.g. `cz1`). |
| **Attrition limit** | Stop fighting that world once your attrition reaches this number. |
| **20/40/60/80/100 %** | Which weakening levels to attack. Lower % costs you less attrition. |
| **Open browser** | Launch the dedicated browser window (do this once per session). |
| **Start / Stop** | Begin or end farming. |

A good starting point: attrition limit **150**, attack **20/40/60 %** only.

## Tips

- **Lower % first.** Attacking 20 % provinces raises your attrition the slowest, so you get
  more fights per day. The app already prefers lower % and central provinces.
- **Leave it running.** It's built for all-day, unattended use. Check back whenever.
- **One browser, many worlds.** All worlds run in the one browser window the app opened.

---

## Troubleshooting

- **"Too many worlds selected."** You enabled more than your plan allows — untick some, or
  upgrade your key.
- **A world never enters GBG.** Make sure you're logged in on that tab and have opened GBG in
  the season at least once. Don't resize the browser window the app opened.
- **Nothing happens after Start.** Confirm the browser window is the one the app opened
  (via **Open browser**), and that you're logged into the worlds you enabled.
- **It stopped fighting a world.** That's normal once the world hits its attrition limit — it's
  done for now.

Still stuck? Send us the app's log window contents and which world/plan you're on.
